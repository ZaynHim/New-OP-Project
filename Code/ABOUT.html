Space World



Space is the vast and endless ocean that surrounds our planet from every direction, looking much like a giant, dark room decorated with millions of twinkling stars. At the center of our neighborhood is our sun, a medium-sized star that warms and lights up the Solar System, which includes our warm, life-giving home Earth alongside Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. .
